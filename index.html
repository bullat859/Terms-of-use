04 Microphone & Voice Assistant Terms

The in-app voice command assistant parses spoken trigger phrases using fully on-device, rule-based voice activity detection (VAD) algorithms to set alarms, launch navigation, trigger gestures, or execute user-defined shortcuts.

  • Local Audio Processing Only: Voice input is never uploaded to remote cloud servers, never retained beyond the active recognition session (RAM-only buffer), never used for behavioral profiling, and never sold or shared with third parties.
  • No Continuous Listening: The microphone is activated only when you explicitly enable voice trigger mode and is automatically deactivated according to your configured timeout settings or when you disable the mode.
  • Audio Focus Management: The App manages audio focus during speech synthesis playback to prevent audio bleed, protecting your listening privacy from unauthorized audio capture during TTS playback.
  • User Responsibility: You are solely responsible for ensuring voice-triggered actions comply with your local eavesdropping, wiretapping, and privacy recording laws when using the App in environments with other individuals present.
